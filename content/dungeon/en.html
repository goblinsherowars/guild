Titan Dungeon Guide

General information

The Dungeon is an unlimited source of gold and Sparks of Power, helping you upgrade heroes much faster. You can spend the gold on every gold-priced item in the Town Shop four times a day, and buy Skin Stones daily in the VIP Shop if you have reached the required VIP level.

The main idea is to prioritize upgrading Water Titans and improve Earth and Fire Titans as needed. The goal is to reach the “infinite Dungeon” stage, where your team can reliably keep progressing.

After that, you can bring your Earth Titans to the maximum level in about two weeks — if you have plenty of free time :)

The maximum difficulty is 218k for Water, Fire, and Earth rooms, and 418k for mixed rooms.

Experience has shown that a Water team with 220k power can clear mixed rooms at maximum difficulty and heal Earth and Water Titans in 80% of rooms.

Healing Titans in mixed rooms

Use a Water team for mixed rooms. The required healing lineup is Sigurd, Tidus, and Hyperion.

While Angus or Moloch is being healed, it is recommended to replace them with Nova, Orm, or Mairi.

Room selection and upgrades

Room priority: Water → Mixed → Earth → Fire.

If no healing is needed, you can clear a mixed room with a team of Water Titans.

Upgrade priority: Sigurd, Tidus, Hyperion, Angus. Upgrade the others as needed.

Key tip: retrying a battle

If a Titan dies during healing or loses even more health instead of recovering:

  1. Press “Retry”.
  2. Pause the game as soon as the battle begins.
  3. Press “Retreat”.
  4. Return to the Dungeon.
  5. Complete the room again with a full Water team.

This lets you avoid losing health and try healing again later.

Additional tips

Replacing Moloch

While Moloch is being healed:

  • Araji can temporarily act as the tank.
  • Alecto can also serve as a temporary tank.

You can upgrade Araji beyond Moloch if your Fire team is already sufficiently developed.

Using Angus

Try to keep Angus’s ultimate ability fully charged at all times.

If Angus’s ultimate is not charged:

  • Play the room manually.
  • Do not let him use his ultimate at the end of the battle.
  • Carry the charged ultimate into the next room.

Upgrade Avalon as a temporary replacement for Angus while he is being healed. A team of four Earth Titans can clear Earth rooms.

This makes long runs of Earth rooms much easier.

Clearing Earth rooms without Angus

  • Pallant can temporarily replace Angus. Upgrade him enough to help the team clear two Earth rooms.
  • Once Avalon is well developed, you can clear Earth rooms with four Titans, without Angus. This lets you keep progressing while Angus recovers health.
