Quick Hydra Guide

The main rule

Do not spread damage across different heads. The guild chooses one head and works together to finish it whenever possible.

Control is mostly ineffective against the Hydra. Heroes with sustained damage, healing, speed boosts, and team buffs are more valuable.

Main lineups

1. Jhu + Mojo

A classic Hydra pairing.

Core: Jhu — Mojo + a tank, healer, and support heroes.

Good additions:

  • Martha — healing and team acceleration.
  • Aidan — healing and improved survivability.
  • Byrna — healing and support.
  • Dorian — vampirism to help the team stay alive.
  • Faceless — useful support, especially alongside Martha.

2. Kendle + Xe’Sha + Byrna

Core: Kendle — Xe’Sha — Byrna + two more heroes.

Good healers

  • Martha
  • Aidan
  • Dorian
  • Maya
  • Morrigan — if you need extra stability.

Good support heroes

  • Faceless
  • Mojo
  • Tempus
  • Sebastian
  • Polaris

Prioritize heroes who help Xe’Sha survive longer and use her abilities more often.

3. Mushy and Shroom

Mushy works especially well in teams with plenty of healing.

A good lineup: Mushy and Shroom — Xe’Sha — Byrna — Martha — Faceless.

Other good additions:

  • Aidan
  • Dorian
  • Mojo
  • Tempus
  • Polaris

The key principle: more healing and longer team survival mean higher total damage.

What to do every day

  1. Check which head the guild is currently attacking.
  2. Use your strongest specialized lineups: Jhu + Mojo; Kendle + Xe’Sha + Byrna; Mushy and Shroom.
  3. Do not break up a strong pairing for one weak attack.
  4. Do not switch to another head if the current one can still be finished.
  5. When choosing between healing and control, a healer or a hero who buffs the team is usually more useful against the Hydra.
